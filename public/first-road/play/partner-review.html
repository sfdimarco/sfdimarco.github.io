Familiar setup · optional

These choices apply when you begin a new encounter. Resume and retry keep your current familiar and finished steps.

Xbox controller
Left stick: move, gently to walk, fully to run.
A: interact · B: dodge · X: send familiar · Y: burst.
LB: recall · RB: ward · Menu: pause.

Keyboard
WASD / arrows: walk · Shift: run · E: interact.
X: dodge · J: send · Q: burst · R: recall · Space: ward.
Escape: pause. Click ground to walk there.

Menus
Stick or D-pad: highlight · A: confirm · B: back.
Left/right adjusts the highlighted choice. Keyboard arrows and Enter also work.

Send chooses pursuit automatically. Recall stops attacks until a new order. No stance cycling required. Leave a locked warning, then use the green handle during recovery.
